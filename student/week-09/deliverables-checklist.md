# Week 9 deliverables checklist

- [ ] Current-state context and dependency/data-flow maps distinguish facts, assumptions, and unknowns
- [ ] API, data, and event contracts have executable characterization evidence
- [ ] ADR compares at least two viable designs
- [ ] Compatibility matrix covers old/new readers and writers
- [ ] Change is split into independently deployable stages
- [ ] Each stage has progression, stop, and rollback evidence
- [ ] Mixed-version and partial-rollout behavior is tested
- [ ] Temporary migration mechanisms have owners and removal conditions
- [ ] Security, operability, performance, and failure risks are recorded
- [ ] Student leads the design review and responds with evidence
