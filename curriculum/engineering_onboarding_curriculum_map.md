# 13-week curriculum map

Expected student time is 8–10 hours weekly: 2 hours reading/lab, 4–5 hours implementation, 1–2 hours testing/evidence, and a 60-minute mentor review. Weeks are cumulative; the capstone reuses API, data, messaging, telemetry, reliability, and operational practices.

| Week | Theme | Environment profile | Primary evidence |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Engineering Foundations and Windows Tooling | `week-01` | Demo, tests, decision log |
| 2 | HTTP APIs and Contracts | `week-02` | Demo, tests, decision log |
| 3 | Data Modeling and SQL | `week-03` | Demo, tests, decision log |
| 4 | Document and Graph Data | `week-04` | Demo, tests, decision log |
| 5 | Caching and Concurrency | `week-05` | Demo, tests, decision log |
| 6 | Events and Messaging | `week-06` | Demo, tests, decision log |

| 8 | Observability | `week-08` | Demo, tests, decision log |
| 9 | Reliability Patterns | `week-09` | Demo, tests, decision log |
| 10 | Security Fundamentals | `week-10` | Demo, tests, decision log |
| 11 | Performance and Capacity | `week-11` | Demo, tests, decision log |
| 12 | Delivery and Operations | `week-12` | Demo, tests, decision log |
| 13 | Capstone Incident | `week-13` | Demo, tests, decision log |

## Gates

- Weeks 1–4: can navigate tools, contracts, and data models.
- Weeks 5–9: can reason about distributed behavior and failure.
- Weeks 10–12: can ship and operate changes responsibly.
- Week 13: can synthesize evidence during an incident.
